Disc images worth burning, picked for people who remember when a CD-R was cutting-edge technology. Every image here may be freely redistributed. The depot only links to each project's own downloads; a monthly job fetches every image, records its size and SHA-256, and Krouton refuses a download that doesn't match.

Console homebrew

Open-source tools for the GameCube, Wii, Dreamcast and PlayStation: boot discs that burn on a CD-R or DVD-R, and SD-card tools for making 1:1 backups of discs you own. No games: bring your own. A Wii only reads burned DVDs with a modchip, and only on the early drive models; DVD+R discs also need their book type set to DVD-ROM, which Krouton can do.

Swiss for GameCube: boot disc

The all-in-one GameCube homebrew utility: it browses SD adapters, memory cards and the network, launches homebrew, and plays your own game backups with video-mode and region fixes. The boot disc image is for a GameCube with a modchip, which is what lets the console read a burned disc.

GameCube discs are 8 cm. Burn to an 8 cm mini DVD-R; a full-size disc doesn't fit the tray.
